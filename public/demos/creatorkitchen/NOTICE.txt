The desk and garden illustrations were authored for this portfolio. Portuguese pronunciation clips were generated locally with macOS text-to-speech (Luciana) from the phrases “Olá, tudo bem?” and “Obrigado”. They contain no original user recordings or third-party uploaded media.

The lesson/ directory contains original lesson text, SVG illustrations, six Brazilian Portuguese pronunciation clips generated locally with macOS Luciana text-to-speech, and a short MP4 made from the authored practice card and its pronunciation audio. No third-party recordings or photographs are included.

Grammar checked against Camões PLE (https://www.instituto-camoes.pt/activity/centro-virtual/referencial-camoes-ple-2), Michaelis verb guidance (https://michaelis.uol.com.br/moderno-portugues/nocoes-gramaticais/verbos/), and Ciberdúvidas on talvez (https://ciberduvidas.iscte-iul.pt/consultorio/perguntas/talvez-seja-o-que-permite-o-acordo/22011). The examples and exercises are newly written, not copied from those references.
